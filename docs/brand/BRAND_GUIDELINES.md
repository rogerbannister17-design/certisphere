# Brand Guidelines

Certisphere is an AI-powered Integrated Management System for building, managing, auditing, and certifying ISO management systems.

Tagline: "Build, manage and certify your management systems in one platform."

## Purpose

These guidelines define how Certisphere appears and communicates across product interfaces, websites, documents, presentations, video, sales collateral, and customer-facing assets.

## Brand Attributes

- Trusted: communicates assurance, governance, and certification readiness.
- Precise: favours clarity, traceability, and evidence over decorative excess.
- Enterprise-ready: suitable for regulated organisations, auditors, consultants, and leadership teams.
- Intelligent: uses AI as an assistant while preserving human accountability.
- Integrated: connects standards, clauses, processes, evidence, risks, audits, suppliers, training, and management review.

## Mandatory Rules

- Use the official brand name `Certisphere`.
- Use the trademark form `Certisphere(TM)` in formal commercial documents where trademark indication is required.
- Preserve the approved tagline exactly.
- Use brand colours from [Colour System](COLOUR_SYSTEM.md).
- Use typography from [Typography](TYPOGRAPHY.md).
- Use logo rules from [Logo Usage](LOGO_USAGE.md).
- Do not introduce unmanaged visual styles for product, marketing, or customer documentation.
- Do not use AI-generated images unless they follow [Illustration Style](ILLUSTRATION_STYLE.md), [Photography Style](PHOTOGRAPHY_STYLE.md), and [Video Style Guide](VIDEO_STYLE_GUIDE.md).
