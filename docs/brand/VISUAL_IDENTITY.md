# Visual Identity

## Identity Summary

Certisphere uses a blue-led enterprise identity designed to communicate trust, assurance, digital intelligence, and certification readiness.

## Visual Characteristics

- Deep navy foundations for authority.
- Bright blue accents for action, intelligence, and progress.
- White and soft grey surfaces for clarity.
- Controlled gradients used as depth cues, not decoration.
- Minimal icons and diagrams that support fast comprehension.
- Generous whitespace and clear hierarchy.

## Composition

Layouts should feel structured, calm, and professional. Product and operational interfaces should prioritise scanning, comparison, task completion, and repeated use.

Marketing compositions may be more expressive but must still preserve clarity and trust. Avoid visual clutter, excessive decorative effects, and unrelated colour palettes.
