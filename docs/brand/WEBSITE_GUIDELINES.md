# Website Guidelines

## Purpose

The Certisphere website must communicate the product category, commercial value, trust posture, and certification-readiness outcome quickly and accurately.

## Website Principles

- Lead with Certisphere as the product.
- State the platform purpose clearly.
- Use the approved tagline.
- Show actual product, diagrams, or governed illustrations rather than generic stock imagery.
- Avoid unsupported certification claims.
- Maintain accessibility, responsive layout, strong contrast, and readable typography.

## Visual Rules

- Use the official colour system.
- Use logo variants appropriate to theme.
- Use generous whitespace.
- Use product screenshots only when current and approved.
- Use customer-facing terminology consistent with [Brand Voice](BRAND_VOICE.md).

## Content Rules

- Tie claims to product capabilities: controlled information, workflow, evidence, audit, risk, suppliers, training, management review, and AI governance.
- Make AI governance explicit: AI suggests, humans approve.
- Do not imply automatic certification.
