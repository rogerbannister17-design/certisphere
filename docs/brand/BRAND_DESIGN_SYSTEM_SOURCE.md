CERTISPHERE™ Brand Design System
This design system defines reusable visual and interaction standards for all CERTISPHERE™ assets—including print, web, video and interactive materials. It ensures that every touchpoint shares a coherent look‑and‑feel that aligns with CERTISPHERE’s values of trust, assurance, innovation and continuous improvement. The guidelines below build on best‑practice research from modern design systems and accessibility requirements【783736926887460†L258-L267】【924004982159976†L40-L47】.
Official colour palette and gradients
CERTISPHERE’s visual identity is built around a blue‑centric colour palette that conveys trust, professionalism and innovation. The palette comprises deep blues for authority, bright azures for innovation, and neutral whites and greys for clarity. Green and teal accents should be avoided to maintain brand consistency.

Gradient guidance
Two main gradient recipes are recommended:
	•	Hero gradient: Midnight Navy (#0B2345) → Azure Blue (#0078D4) → Bright Sky Blue (#29A9FF). Use for cover backgrounds, hero sections or motion backgrounds.
	•	Surface gradient: Pure White (#FFFFFF) → Soft Grey (#F5F7FA). Use for cards, panels and interface backgrounds where subtle separation is required.
Ensure gradients run in a single direction (top‑to‑bottom or left‑to‑right) and maintain smooth transitions. Do not introduce teal or green hues.
Contrast and accessibility
Text and interactive elements must meet WCAG 2.2 contrast standards【924004982159976†L40-L47】. This means a minimum contrast ratio of 4.5:1 for normal text against backgrounds, and 3:1 for large or bold text. Use colour pairings such as Midnight Navy on white, Azure Blue on white, or white on Midnight Navy. Logos and purely decorative text are exempt【924004982159976†L40-L57】.
Typography hierarchy
Typography conveys hierarchy, tone and readability. CERTISPHERE uses Aptos for digital applications. When typesetting PDFs or printed materials, Avenir (or Segoe UI if Avenir is unavailable) may be used. Both fonts pair geometric forms with modern legibility, reflecting our technological focus.

Hierarchy and emphasis
Headlines should be bold and use the darkest colours (Midnight Navy) to convey authority. Subheadings may use Corporate Navy. Body text uses dark grey (#1F2937) or Midnight Navy at 85 % opacity. Emphasize important words using Aptos Semibold or italic. Avoid using underlines except for links, which should appear in Azure Blue with an underline on hover.
Iconography style
Icons serve as visual anchors for concepts like AI, governance, workflow and security. Following guidelines from Athennian’s brand iconography【451322905967848†L19-L21】, CERTISPHERE icons should:
	•	Use a minimal colour palette—only the primary and accent blues. Icons can reverse to white on dark backgrounds.
	•	Be flat, with little to no depth; avoid gradients within icons.
	•	Use simple, identifiable shapes that clearly represent the concept (e.g., a shield for security, network nodes for collaboration).
	•	Use consistent stroke widths (e.g., 2 px for a 24 px icon) and rounded corner caps.
	•	Integrate naturally within layouts; do not float icons without accompanying labels【451322905967848†L23-L25】.
Icon sizes should scale in multiples of 4 px (16 px, 20 px, 24 px, 32 px). Use solid fills only for special states (e.g., active notifications) and outline style for default states.
Illustration rules
Illustrations communicate complex ideas like compliance workflows and AI‑enabled certification. Inspired by Pixelmatters’ advice【430696800832688†L69-L81】, our illustration guidelines emphasise:
	•	Narrative and values: Illustrations should highlight our core values—precision, transparency, collaboration and continual improvement【430696800832688†L96-L112】. Depict abstract concepts through metaphors (e.g., interconnected nodes for collaboration). Avoid literal depictions of people to keep focus on universal ideas.
	•	Defined style: Use vector‑based artwork with a consistent stroke weight and geometric simplicity. Apply soft shadows and glassmorphism effects for depth. Limit colours to the official palette; only white, Midnight Navy, Corporate Navy, Azure Blue and Bright Sky Blue may appear in the artwork. Gradients should be subtle and avoid adding new hues.
	•	Work‑in‑progress aesthetic: Illustrations may show layers, outlines or grids to signal continual improvement【430696800832688†L115-L121】.
	•	Guideline documentation: Specify stroke sizes, shadow direction and light sources. For example, default stroke: 2 px with square caps; light source: top‑left with 15° elevation. Being explicit removes ambiguity【430696800832688†L125-L139】.
	•	Accessibility: Provide alt text for every illustration describing the concept. Ensure enough contrast between illustration elements and their backgrounds.
Motion graphics language
Motion should guide the viewer and create delightful, purposeful transitions. Webflow’s motion principles emphasise constructing narratives layer by layer, smooth transitions, and accessibility【262132255932641†L759-L792】. Adapted for CERTISPHERE:
	•	Purposeful motion – Use animation to build content gradually: reveal elements one at a time to imply growth, transformation and emergence【262132255932641†L771-L776】. This reinforces the idea of continuous improvement.
	•	Enhance focus – Zoom, crop or pan to highlight the main idea【262132255932641†L780-L785】. Use slow camera moves to draw attention to key data or features.
	•	Reduce friction – Transitions should be smooth and legible; avoid jarring cuts. When combining 3D and 2D elements, plan motions in storyboard form to maintain coherence【262132255932641†L789-L793】.
	•	Consistent timing – Motions should follow a duration range of 0.4–0.8 seconds for micro‑interactions (e.g., button hovers) and 6–12 seconds for macro scenes (e.g., introductory sequences). Use easing curves (ease‑in‑out) to simulate natural acceleration and deceleration.
	•	Brand colours and effects – Apply soft blue glows and glassmorphic overlays. Avoid sudden colour shifts; transitions should fade between official palette colours.
	•	Accessibility – Provide an option to reduce motion. Avoid fast flashing or strobe effects.

UI components
A robust design system includes reusable components with defined properties and behaviours【662803134275550†L15-L21】【662803134275550†L61-L66】. Each component should provide variants, sizes and states【662803134275550†L73-L80】. Below are guidelines for core components tailored to CERTISPHERE.
Buttons
Primary buttons use Azure Blue (#0078D4) with white text. Secondary buttons use Corporate Navy (#143D73) for darker interfaces or Bright Sky Blue (#29A9FF) for lighter contexts. Ghost buttons have transparent backgrounds with Midnight Navy borders. All buttons have 4 px corner radius, 16 px vertical padding and 24 px horizontal padding. States:
	•	Default: Solid fill with drop shadow of 2 px blur, 25 % opacity using the same hue.
	•	Hover: Slightly darker shade of the fill colour; maintain text colour contrast.
	•	Active: Scale down by 2 %; remove shadow; darken fill further.
	•	Focused: Add a 2 px outline in Bright Sky Blue (#29A9FF).
	•	Disabled: Use Soft Grey (#F5F7FA) fill and Corporate Navy text at 30 % opacity.
Text inputs
Text fields use white or soft grey backgrounds with 1 px Midnight Navy borders. On focus, border colour transitions to Azure Blue. Labels sit above the field in 14 px Aptos and Corporate Navy. Provide helper text below in 12 px text. Validation messages appear in red (#C53030) or green (#2F855A) with icon feedback.
Checkboxes and radio buttons
Boxes and radio circles are 20 px with 2 px corners. The unchecked state is defined by a 2 px Corporate Navy outline on a white background. When checked, the fill is Azure Blue and the checkmark is white. Provide 8 px spacing between the control and its label.
Toggles (switches)
Switches use a 32 px × 20 px track. The off state uses Soft Grey; the on state uses Bright Sky Blue. The thumb is white with a subtle drop shadow. Labels indicate the state (On/Off) and should update immediately when toggled.
Dropdown menus (select)
Select controls have similar styling to text inputs. The arrow icon uses Azure Blue and rotates on open. Use search functionality for long lists. Highlight hovered options with Soft Grey and selected options with Bright Sky Blue.
Cards
Cards provide modular containers for information. Use Soft Grey backgrounds on white pages and white backgrounds on coloured panels. Cards have 8 px corner radius, 16 px internal padding and can include a header, body and footer. Variants:
	•	Standard: Slight drop shadow (0 px 4 px 10 px rgba(11,35,69,0.05)).
	•	Elevated: Larger shadow for emphasis; use for callouts or modals.
	•	Outlined: 1 px border in Soft Grey for subtle separation when stacking cards.
Modals (dialogs)
Modals overlay a dark semi‑transparent backdrop (#122844, 70 % opacity). The modal container uses white or Soft Grey backgrounds with 24 px corner radius and a drop shadow. Provide clear close buttons and ensure focus is trapped within the modal【662803134275550†L161-L166】.
Navigation
Use a horizontal navigation bar with Midnight Navy background and white text. Active links use Azure Blue underlines. For sidebars, use Corporate Navy backgrounds with collapse/expand indicators.
Alerts and notifications
Alerts come in four variants: info (Azure Blue), success (green #2F855A), warning (yellow #D69E2E) and error (red #C53030). Use icons to differentiate types. Alerts should be dismissible and accessible.
Data tables
Tables should be responsive. Use Soft Grey stripes for alternating rows. Headers use Bold Aptos at 14 px and Midnight Navy fill. Provide sorting and pagination controls. Ensure row hover states use Bright Sky Blue tints for clarity.
Page templates
To maintain consistency across marketing materials, presentations and documentation, standard page templates should be used. Templates are built around generous whitespace, clear hierarchy and modular sections.
Cover page
The cover page features a dark hero panel with a Hero gradient background and a central area reserved for the title. Include a glowing digital globe or network illustration as seen in the sample cover (see accompanying image) and overlay it with translucent icons representing AI, governance and certification. Use large H1 text in white or Bright Sky Blue.
Section page
Section pages use a white background with a coloured header bar in Corporate Navy or Azure Blue. H2 headings sit at the top left. Use 12 % tinted backgrounds behind key callouts. Include sidebars or callout boxes in Soft Grey with subtle icons.
Content page
Content pages contain body text, diagrams, charts and tables. Use multi‑column layouts (two or three columns) for long reports. Charts should use the official palette with distinct blues and neutrals. Ensure sufficient margin on all sides (minimum 2rem on mobile, 4rem on desktop)【783736926887460†L369-L373】.
Footer
Footers use Midnight Navy backgrounds with small white text. Include navigation links, social icons and legal information. Icons invert to white when placed on dark backgrounds.

Implementation notes and maintenance
This design system is a living document. Review it periodically to ensure it aligns with emerging accessibility standards and design trends. Provide visual examples (e.g., the colour palette and recoloured cover illustration included in this project) to help designers understand the aesthetic. Use a shared documentation platform (such as Notion or Figma) to make the guidelines accessible and keep components up‑to‑date【430696800832688†L146-L150】.
To maintain consistency across teams:
	•	Use a component library built using Atomic Design principles【662803134275550†L61-L66】. Define atoms (buttons, icons), molecules (form fields) and organisms (dialogs, navigation) so that designers and developers work from the same building blocks.
	•	Document variants, states and sizes for each component【662803134275550†L73-L80】. Provide code snippets and usage examples.
	•	Enforce accessibility: ensure adequate contrast, keyboard navigation, focus states and ARIA labels.
	•	Encourage feedback: designers should log issues and propose improvements. The design system should evolve with CERTISPHERE’s product and brand growth.
