# Illustration Style

## Purpose

Illustrations help explain governance, certification readiness, traceability, AI assistance, and integrated management systems.

## Style Rules

- Use vector-style geometry with clean lines.
- Use the official colour palette only.
- Use soft shadows and blue glow sparingly for depth.
- Avoid literal depictions of people unless explicitly approved for a campaign.
- Represent complexity through systems, nodes, panels, flows, controlled documents, shields, and standards.
- Leave space for typography when illustrations are used as hero or cover assets.
- Provide alt text for every illustration.

## Common Motifs

- Digital globe with connected organisations.
- Knowledge graph nodes linked to clauses, documents, evidence, and risks.
- Governed workflow paths from draft to approval to published.
- Compliance dashboard panels with evidence and audit indicators.
- Shield and certificate forms for assurance.

## AI Image Generation

AI-generated illustrations must specify brand colours by name or hex value, prohibit embedded text, and preserve negative space for approved typography.
