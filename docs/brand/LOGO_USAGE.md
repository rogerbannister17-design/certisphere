# Logo Usage

## Primary Logo

Use the approved Certisphere logo as the primary brand mark across product, documents, website, presentations, and commercial assets.

## Logo Variants

- Full-colour logo for light backgrounds.
- Light logo for dark backgrounds.
- Dark logo for light, low-colour, or formal document contexts.
- Favicon and app icon variants for browser and device surfaces.

## Usage Rules

- Preserve logo proportions.
- Do not stretch, crop, rotate, recolour, outline, or add effects to the logo.
- Maintain clear space around the mark equal to at least the height of the logo symbol.
- Do not place the logo on busy images or low-contrast backgrounds.
- Use light and dark logo variants according to background theme.
- Do not recreate the logo from text.

## Minimum Sizes

- Digital header logo: 120 px wide minimum.
- Favicon: use approved favicon assets only.
- Presentation cover logo: 180 px wide minimum unless layout constraints require otherwise.

## Asset Location

Logo assets are governed under [Brand Assets](BRAND_ASSETS.md) and `docs/brand/assets/logos/`.
