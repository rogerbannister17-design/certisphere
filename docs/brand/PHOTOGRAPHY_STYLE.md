# Photography Style

## Purpose

Photography should support trust, professionalism, operational credibility, and enterprise adoption.

## Approved Subjects

- Professional workspaces.
- Audit and review settings.
- Manufacturing, service, healthcare, education, construction, and technology environments where management-system work is credible.
- Abstract close-ups of documentation, devices, process boards, or quality controls.

## Rules

- Prefer natural, well-lit images.
- Avoid staged stock-photo cliches.
- Avoid exaggerated emotion or unrealistic office scenes.
- Use authentic operational context where possible.
- Do not use imagery that implies certification outcomes are guaranteed.
- Apply navy or blue overlays only when needed for text legibility.

## Accessibility

All photography used in digital content must have meaningful alt text unless decorative.
