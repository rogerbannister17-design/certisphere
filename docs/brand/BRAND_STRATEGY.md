# Brand Strategy

## Positioning

Certisphere is the commercial SaaS platform for organisations that need to build, manage, and certify management systems with confidence.

## Strategic Promise

Certisphere turns management-system work into connected, auditable, evidence-driven operating practice.

## Audience

- Quality managers responsible for ISO 9001 systems.
- Compliance and governance teams managing controlled information.
- Consultants implementing management systems for clients.
- Auditors reviewing evidence, clauses, processes, and approvals.
- Executives needing management review, risk visibility, and certification readiness.

## Differentiation

- Integrated management-system model instead of isolated document storage.
- Controlled information lifecycle with approval, revision, publication, and retention.
- Evidence-first traceability across standards, processes, audits, risks, training, suppliers, and management review.
- AI assistance governed by human review and approval.
- Multi-standard architecture prepared for ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001, ISO 27001, and future standards.

## Commercial Use

Brand strategy must be reflected consistently in sales collateral, onboarding material, product UI, proposal documents, investor material, customer training, and certification-facing reports.
