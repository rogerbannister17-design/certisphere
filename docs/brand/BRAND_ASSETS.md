# Brand Assets

## Asset Governance

Brand assets are governed engineering and commercial assets. They must be stored, reviewed, and version-controlled with the same care as product documentation.

## Asset Structure

- `assets/logos/`: logo variants and usage-specific logo files.
- `assets/icons/`: approved icon sets and source files.
- `assets/illustrations/`: governed illustration source and exports.
- `assets/presentations/`: slide templates and presentation source files.
- `assets/templates/`: Word, PDF, report, and document templates.
- `assets/videos/`: storyboards, motion assets, and approved video source material.
- `assets/colours/`: colour swatches, palette exports, and design-token references.

## Rules

- Do not store unmanaged copies outside `docs/brand`.
- Do not duplicate identical assets under multiple names.
- Use descriptive lowercase filenames with hyphens.
- Preserve original source files where commercially or legally useful.
- Add a README to every asset category folder.
