# Colour System

## Primary Palette

| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | Midnight Navy | `#0B2345` | Headings, dark surfaces, hero panels, authority moments. |
| Primary | Corporate Navy | `#143D73` | Navigation, footers, secondary dark surfaces, outlines. |
| Accent | Azure Blue | `#0078D4` | Primary actions, links, icons, active states. |
| Accent | Bright Sky Blue | `#29A9FF` | Highlights, charts, progress indicators, glows. |
| Neutral | Pure White | `#FFFFFF` | Main backgrounds and document surfaces. |
| Neutral | Soft Grey | `#F5F7FA` | Secondary panels, card backgrounds, form surfaces. |

## Supporting Semantic Colours

Semantic colours may be used only for product state and accessibility:

- Error: `#C53030`
- Warning: `#D69E2E`
- Success: `#2F855A`

These colours must not become dominant brand colours.

## Gradient Recipes

Hero gradient: `#0B2345` to `#0078D4` to `#29A9FF`.

Surface gradient: `#FFFFFF` to `#F5F7FA`.

Gradients must be subtle, directional, and purposeful. Do not introduce teal, green, purple, orange, or decorative multi-hue gradients into the brand system.

## Accessibility

Text and interactive elements must meet WCAG 2.2 AA contrast expectations. Use tested pairings such as Midnight Navy on white, white on Midnight Navy, and Azure Blue with adequate contrast.
