# Brand Review Checklist

This checklist is the mandatory review gate for Certisphere brand, marketing, product, sales, presentation, video, website, and document assets before publication or customer use.

Use this checklist with:

- [Brand Guidelines](BRAND_GUIDELINES.md)
- [Brand Strategy](BRAND_STRATEGY.md)
- [Brand Voice](BRAND_VOICE.md)
- [Visual Identity](VISUAL_IDENTITY.md)
- [Colour System](COLOUR_SYSTEM.md)
- [Typography](TYPOGRAPHY.md)
- [Logo Usage](LOGO_USAGE.md)
- [UI Branding](UI_BRANDING.md)
- [Brand Assets](BRAND_ASSETS.md)

## Review Outcome

Every reviewed asset must receive one outcome:

- Approved: asset can be published or used externally.
- Approved with Conditions: asset can be used only after listed corrections are completed.
- Rejected: asset must not be published or used externally.

## Asset Information

- [ ] Asset name is recorded.
- [ ] Asset owner is recorded.
- [ ] Intended audience is recorded.
- [ ] Intended channel is recorded.
- [ ] Publication or use date is recorded.
- [ ] Review decision and reviewer are recorded.

## Brand Identity

- [ ] The asset uses the approved `Certisphere` name.
- [ ] The approved tagline is used exactly when included.
- [ ] The asset positions Certisphere as an AI-powered Integrated Management System.
- [ ] The asset does not imply certification is guaranteed.
- [ ] The asset does not describe AI as making final compliance decisions.
- [ ] The asset is consistent with [Brand Strategy](BRAND_STRATEGY.md).

## Logo Usage

- [ ] The approved logo asset is used.
- [ ] Logo proportions are preserved.
- [ ] Logo clear space is maintained.
- [ ] Logo contrast is sufficient.
- [ ] The correct light, dark, or full-colour logo variant is used.
- [ ] The logo is not stretched, cropped, recoloured, outlined, rotated, or modified.
- [ ] The logo is not placed on a visually busy or low-contrast background.

## Colour System

- [ ] Colours use the approved palette in [Colour System](COLOUR_SYSTEM.md).
- [ ] Dominant colours remain Midnight Navy, Corporate Navy, Azure Blue, Bright Sky Blue, Pure White, and Soft Grey.
- [ ] Semantic colours are used only for product state or accessibility.
- [ ] Gradients follow the approved hero or surface recipes.
- [ ] Teal, green, purple, orange, and unrelated decorative palettes are not introduced.
- [ ] Text and interactive elements meet WCAG 2.2 AA contrast expectations.

## Typography

- [ ] Typography follows [Typography](TYPOGRAPHY.md).
- [ ] Aptos or an approved fallback typeface is used.
- [ ] Heading hierarchy is clear and consistent.
- [ ] Body text is readable and appropriately sized.
- [ ] Line lengths are suitable for the medium.
- [ ] Underlines are reserved for links.
- [ ] Emphasis uses weight before extra colours.

## Brand Voice

- [ ] Copy follows [Brand Voice](BRAND_VOICE.md).
- [ ] Language is clear, professional, practical, and assured.
- [ ] Claims are specific and supportable.
- [ ] Compliance claims are evidence-led.
- [ ] UK English spelling is used in governed documents.
- [ ] Terminology is consistent with Certisphere product governance.
- [ ] Acronyms are expanded on first use where needed.

## Visual Style

- [ ] Layout feels structured, calm, and professional.
- [ ] Whitespace is intentional.
- [ ] Visual hierarchy supports quick comprehension.
- [ ] Icons follow [Iconography](ICONOGRAPHY.md).
- [ ] Illustrations follow [Illustration Style](ILLUSTRATION_STYLE.md).
- [ ] Photography follows [Photography Style](PHOTOGRAPHY_STYLE.md).
- [ ] Visuals do not feel generic, stock-like, or disconnected from management-system work.

## Product UI Branding

Use this section for product screens, prototypes intended for implementation, and UI screenshots.

- [ ] UI follows [UI Branding](UI_BRANDING.md).
- [ ] UI is appropriate for operational SaaS use.
- [ ] Navigation, controls, buttons, forms, alerts, and tables are visually consistent.
- [ ] Permission, loading, empty, error, and disabled states are represented where relevant.
- [ ] Accessibility expectations are considered.
- [ ] Screens do not introduce unofficial product names or unsupported workflows.

## Documents and Reports

Use this section for Word, PDF, exported reports, audit packs, certificates, proposals, and internal documents.

- [ ] Document follows [Document Templates](DOCUMENT_TEMPLATES.md).
- [ ] Title, date, owner, version, status, and classification are present where relevant.
- [ ] Footer, copyright, and confidentiality language are appropriate.
- [ ] Tables and diagrams are legible.
- [ ] Exported documents preserve logo quality and typography.
- [ ] Evidence, audit, or certification-facing documents avoid unsupported claims.

## Presentations

Use this section for sales decks, investor decks, training decks, and internal presentations.

- [ ] Presentation follows [Presentation Guidelines](PRESENTATION_GUIDELINES.md).
- [ ] Cover slide uses approved logo, title, and brand composition.
- [ ] Slide hierarchy is clear.
- [ ] Slides avoid excessive text.
- [ ] Diagrams and visuals are aligned to the approved brand style.
- [ ] Speaker-facing claims are supportable and consistent with product governance.

## Website and Marketing

- [ ] Website or campaign follows [Website Guidelines](WEBSITE_GUIDELINES.md).
- [ ] The first viewport clearly identifies Certisphere.
- [ ] Product value is communicated without unsupported certification claims.
- [ ] Calls to action are clear.
- [ ] Imagery is relevant to management-system, audit, evidence, or SaaS workflows.
- [ ] Metadata, social previews, and page titles use approved brand language.

## Video and Motion

- [ ] Video follows [Video Style Guide](VIDEO_STYLE_GUIDE.md).
- [ ] Motion is purposeful and not distracting.
- [ ] Captions are provided for spoken content.
- [ ] Visuals respect the official colour system.
- [ ] No fast flashing, strobing, or inaccessible motion is used.
- [ ] AI-generated motion or imagery follows approved prompts and brand constraints.

## Asset Governance

- [ ] Source assets are stored in `docs/brand/assets`.
- [ ] File names are descriptive, lowercase, and hyphenated where practical.
- [ ] No duplicate unmanaged asset copies are introduced.
- [ ] Asset ownership and intended use are clear.
- [ ] External licensed material has permission or licence evidence.
- [ ] AI-generated assets are labelled and reviewed before use.

## Accessibility

- [ ] Text contrast meets WCAG 2.2 AA expectations.
- [ ] Important meaning is not conveyed by colour alone.
- [ ] Images have alt text where used digitally.
- [ ] Documents use meaningful headings.
- [ ] Tables have clear headers.
- [ ] Video has captions where spoken content exists.
- [ ] Motion-sensitive users are considered.

## Legal and Commercial Risk

- [ ] Trademark usage is appropriate.
- [ ] Copyright ownership or licence status is clear.
- [ ] Customer names, logos, screenshots, and data are approved for use.
- [ ] Security, compliance, and certification claims are reviewed.
- [ ] No confidential customer or internal information is exposed.
- [ ] The asset is suitable for commercial SaaS use.
