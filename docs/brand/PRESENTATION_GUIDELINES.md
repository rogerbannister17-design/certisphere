# Presentation Guidelines

## Purpose

Presentations must communicate Certisphere as a commercial SaaS platform for governed, AI-assisted management-system work.

## Structure

- Cover slide with logo, title, tagline, and controlled visual treatment.
- Problem slide focused on uncontrolled information, audit pressure, evidence gaps, or fragmented tools.
- Platform slide showing integrated controlled information, workflow, evidence, audit, risk, training, and AI governance.
- Value slide tied to traceability, certification readiness, and reduced operational friction.
- Proof slide using product evidence, screenshots, architecture, roadmap, or customer outcomes where available.
- Closing slide with clear next action.

## Visual Rules

- Use approved colours, type, and logo placement.
- Keep slide layouts spacious.
- Avoid overloading slides with paragraphs.
- Prefer diagrams, short statements, and evidence-led examples.
- Use icons from the approved iconography style.

## Assets

Presentation assets are governed under `docs/brand/assets/presentations/`.
