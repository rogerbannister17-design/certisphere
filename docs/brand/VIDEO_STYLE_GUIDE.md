# Video Style Guide

## Purpose

Video content should explain Certisphere clearly, build trust, and show how management-system work becomes controlled, traceable, and audit-ready.

## Motion Principles

- Use purposeful motion to reveal structure and relationships.
- Build scenes layer by layer.
- Use smooth transitions and calm pacing.
- Use glows and gradients only within the official colour system.
- Avoid fast flashing, strobing, or distracting movement.
- Provide captions for spoken content.
- Respect reduced-motion requirements in product or web contexts.

## Common Scenes

- Introductory network scene with a digital globe and governed system connections.
- Feature highlight with product panels and controlled information flows.
- Process walkthrough with connected lifecycle nodes.
- Outro scene with logo, tagline, and call to action.
