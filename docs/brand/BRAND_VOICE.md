# Brand Voice

## Voice Principles

- Clear: explain complex management-system work in plain language.
- Assured: communicate confidence without exaggeration.
- Practical: focus on useful actions, evidence, and outcomes.
- Professional: suitable for auditors, executives, consultants, and regulated customers.
- Human-accountable: AI is described as assistance, never autonomous compliance authority.

## Tone By Context

Product UI: concise, calm, task-focused.

Documentation: precise, instructional, and auditable.

Marketing: confident, outcome-led, and specific.

Training: supportive, direct, and workflow-oriented.

Audit-facing material: factual, traceable, and neutral.

## Writing Rules

- Prefer active voice.
- Use `organisation`, `authorisation`, and other UK English spelling in governed documents.
- Avoid vague compliance claims that are not linked to evidence.
- Do not imply certification is guaranteed.
- Do not describe AI as making final compliance decisions.
- Use the tagline exactly when used in full.

## Approved Message Themes

- Build controlled information once and reuse it across standards.
- Make evidence traceable before audit pressure arrives.
- Keep humans accountable while AI accelerates routine analysis.
- Replace uncontrolled shared-drive practice with governed lifecycle control.
