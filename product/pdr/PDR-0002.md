# PDR-0002: Why AI Always Requires Human Approval

Status: Accepted

## Context

Certisphere will use AI to assist users with drafting, summarising, comparing, classifying, reviewing, and suggesting improvements to management-system content. These activities can affect controlled information, evidence interpretation, audit preparation, risk decisions, and certification-facing material.

Management-system accountability must remain with authorised people. ISO-aligned governance depends on clear responsibility, approval, competence, evidence, and traceable decision-making.

## Decision

AI in Certisphere will always require human approval before its output can become controlled information, be published, close a workflow, approve evidence, dispose records, close findings, approve CAPA, or make certification-facing claims.

AI output follows this governed path:

AI suggestion -> human review -> approval -> workflow -> published or rejected outcome.

## Rationale

Human approval protects customer accountability, auditability, trust, and certification integrity. AI can accelerate work, but it must not replace authorised decision-making for controlled management-system outcomes.

This decision preserves:

- Human accountability for controlled information.
- Traceable review and approval history.
- Clear separation between suggestion and approved content.
- Evidence-backed decision-making.
- Protection against hallucinated, incomplete, biased, or unsupported AI outputs.
- Alignment with brand and commercial claim governance.

## Consequences

- AI suggestions must be stored or represented separately from approved controlled information until reviewed.
- AI actions must be auditable.
- User interfaces must clearly distinguish draft AI suggestions from approved content.
- APIs and application services must prevent AI from directly publishing or approving controlled information.
- Workflows must include authorised human review where AI output is used.
- AI-generated brand, document, compliance, or certification-facing content must pass human review before use.

## Alternatives Considered

### AI directly updates controlled information

Rejected. It would undermine accountability, approval history, auditability, and customer trust.

### AI directly approves low-risk changes

Rejected. Low-risk categories change over time and can still affect controlled information, evidence, or certification-facing records.

### AI only as an external drafting tool

Rejected. It would reduce product value and traceability. Certisphere should support AI assistance inside governed workflows, but with human approval.
