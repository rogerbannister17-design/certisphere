# PDR-0004: Why Consultants Are First-class Users

Status: Accepted

## Context

Many Certisphere customers will build or maintain management systems with help from consultants. Consultants may support multiple clients, bring implementation templates, coordinate evidence collection, advise on certification readiness, and help customers sustain management-system operation after certification.

If consultants are treated as occasional external users, the product would fail to support a major route to market and a major operational workflow for small and medium-sized organisations.

## Decision

Consultants are first-class users in Certisphere.

The product must support consultant identities, organisation memberships, scoped access, multi-client working patterns, controlled collaboration, and auditable contribution to management-system work.

## Rationale

Consultants are not merely guests. They often act as implementation partners, advisors, reviewers, trainers, evidence coordinators, and external process facilitators. Making them first-class users supports customer success and commercial adoption.

This decision enables:

- Consultant portal experiences.
- Multi-client access without cross-tenant data leakage.
- Scoped permissions per organisation.
- Auditable consultant activity.
- Reusable implementation patterns.
- Client progress oversight.
- Better onboarding for organisations preparing for first certification.

## Consequences

- Identity and organisation membership models must support users belonging to multiple organisations.
- Role-based access control must distinguish consultant capabilities from customer administrator, owner, auditor, and standard user roles.
- Audit trails must clearly show consultant actions.
- Product workflows must support consultant review and preparation work while preserving customer accountability for approval.
- Commercial packaging may include consultant-led or partner-enabled operating models.

## Alternatives Considered

### Consultants as temporary guests

Rejected. Temporary guest access does not support multi-client work, recurring advisory relationships, or repeatable implementation workflows.

### Consultants sharing customer accounts

Rejected. It would violate security, auditability, accountability, and commercial SaaS operating standards.

### Consultants outside the platform

Rejected. It would fragment evidence, advice, approvals, and implementation history across email, spreadsheets, and external documents.
