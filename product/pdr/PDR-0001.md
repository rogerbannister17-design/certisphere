# PDR-0001: Why Certisphere Supports Multi-standard Management From Day One

Status: Accepted

## Context

Certisphere is an AI-powered Integrated Management System for building, managing, auditing, and certifying ISO management systems. Target customers may begin with ISO 9001, but many will later extend their management system to ISO 14001, ISO 45001, ISO 27001, or sector-specific standards.

Management-system standards share common patterns: policy, leadership, planning, support, operation, performance evaluation, improvement, documented information, evidence, audit, corrective action, risk, competence, and management review. If Certisphere is designed around a single standard, customers would be forced to duplicate processes, evidence, and controlled information when they adopt additional standards.

## Decision

Certisphere will support multi-standard management from day one.

The product model must treat ISO standards, clauses, processes, evidence, risks, audits, records, workflows, and controlled information as reusable, linkable product objects rather than standard-specific silos.

## Rationale

Multi-standard support is central to the product vision. Certisphere is not intended to be a narrow ISO 9001 document repository. It is an Integrated Management System platform that supports organisations as their management-system maturity grows.

Designing for multi-standard operation from the start protects the domain model from later rework and enables:

- Shared controlled information across multiple standards.
- Single evidence records mapped to multiple clauses.
- Combined internal audit programmes.
- Integrated management review.
- Combined risk and opportunity management.
- Scalable certification readiness reporting.
- Consultant-led implementation across different client standards.

## Consequences

- Domain models must avoid hard-coded ISO 9001 assumptions.
- Clause mappings must support multiple standards and revisions.
- Reports must distinguish standard-specific conformance from shared evidence.
- Workflows must allow standard-specific approvals where required without duplicating the underlying controlled information.
- Search, filtering, dashboards, and audit views must support multiple management-system scopes.
- Release planning must preserve future ISO 14001, ISO 45001, ISO 27001, and additional standard compatibility.

## Alternatives Considered

### ISO 9001-only foundation

Rejected. It would simplify early delivery but create structural rework when introducing environmental, occupational health and safety, information security, or integrated management-system features.

### Separate modules for each standard

Rejected. It would encourage duplication of procedures, evidence, risks, audits, and management reviews, reducing the value of an integrated platform.

### Generic document management with optional clause tags

Rejected. It would not provide enough structure for evidence traceability, audit readiness, workflow control, or integrated management-system reporting.
