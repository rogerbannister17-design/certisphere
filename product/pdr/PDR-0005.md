# PDR-0005: Why Certification Bodies Receive Dedicated Portal Access

Status: Accepted

## Context

Certification bodies and auditors need access to current controlled information, objective evidence, audit trails, management review outputs, findings, corrective actions, and certification-relevant records. In many organisations this evidence is gathered manually into folders, spreadsheets, and email packs before an audit.

Certisphere can reduce audit preparation effort by giving certification bodies controlled, scoped, read-only or workflow-specific access to the information they need.

## Decision

Certification bodies receive dedicated portal access in Certisphere.

Access must be scoped, permission-controlled, tenant-aware, time-bound where appropriate, and fully audited. Certification body users must not receive broad internal user access by default.

## Rationale

Dedicated portal access improves audit readiness while protecting customer control. Certification body users have different needs from employees, consultants, administrators, and internal auditors.

This decision enables:

- Clear separation between operational users and certification-facing users.
- Read-only access to current published controlled information where appropriate.
- Evidence review without uncontrolled export packs.
- Better audit trail visibility.
- Reduced customer preparation burden.
- Future certification-body workflows such as evidence requests, audit sampling, findings, and surveillance preparation.

## Consequences

- Identity and authorisation must support certification-body roles and scoped access.
- Audit trails must record certification-body access and actions.
- Evidence and controlled information views must distinguish current, superseded, archived, and restricted content.
- Customer administrators must control what certification bodies can see.
- APIs and UI must avoid exposing unnecessary operational or sensitive information.
- Future portal work must preserve independence, confidentiality, and scope boundaries.

## Alternatives Considered

### Export-only audit packs

Rejected as the sole approach. Export packs may still be useful, but they can become stale, duplicate controlled information, and weaken traceability.

### Certification bodies as normal organisation users

Rejected. Certification-body access requires narrower scope, different workflows, and stronger audit visibility than typical internal users.

### No certification-body access

Rejected. It would miss a core opportunity to reduce audit preparation effort and strengthen evidence traceability.
