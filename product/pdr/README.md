# Product Decision Records

Product Decision Records document permanent product decisions for Certisphere. They explain why a product direction exists, what alternatives were considered, and what consequences future releases must preserve.

PDRs are complementary to architecture decision records. ADRs govern technical architecture. PDRs govern product intent, commercial positioning, user model, lifecycle expectations, and product constraints.

## Governance

New PDRs must include:

- Status.
- Context.
- Decision.
- Rationale.
- Consequences.
- Alternatives considered.
- Related governance documents.

Accepted PDRs are binding until superseded by a later accepted PDR.
