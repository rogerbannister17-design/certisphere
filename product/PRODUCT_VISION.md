# Product Vision

## Mission

Certisphere helps organisations build, manage, audit, and certify ISO management systems in one governed platform.

## Product Vision

Certisphere is an AI-powered Integrated Management System designed to build, manage, and certify ISO management systems. It gives organisations a controlled operating environment for policies, procedures, forms, records, evidence, risk, supplier assurance, audits, management review, training, and continuous improvement.

Tagline:

> Build, manage and certify your management systems in one platform.

## Product Principles

- Controlled by design: controlled information must have ownership, revision control, approval, publication status, audit history, and retention rules.
- Human accountable: AI may suggest improvements, but humans review and approve all changes to controlled information.
- Evidence-led: every workflow should produce usable evidence for certification, assurance, and management review.
- Multi-standard: the product must support ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 27001, and future standards without forcing duplicate systems.
- Multi-tenant: every customer must be isolated by organisation and governed by role-based access control.
- Explainable: decisions, approvals, audit outcomes, and AI suggestions must be traceable and understandable.
- Commercial-grade: features must be secure, testable, observable, documented, and supportable.

## Target Markets

- Small and medium-sized organisations preparing for first ISO certification.
- Multi-site organisations maintaining several management standards.
- Consultants delivering management system implementation and maintenance.
- Certification bodies and auditors reviewing evidence, findings, and corrective actions.
- Regulated suppliers that must demonstrate operational control and continual improvement.

## Customer Personas

### Quality Manager

Owns the management system, document control, audit programme, evidence, corrective actions, risks, and management review reporting. Needs a practical system that reduces spreadsheet and shared-drive overhead.

### Operations Leader

Needs clear ownership of processes, actions, evidence, and improvement work without learning certification jargon.

### Consultant

Supports multiple clients and needs repeatable implementation patterns, document control, review workflows, and client progress visibility.

### Internal Auditor

Plans audits, records evidence, raises findings, and tracks corrective actions through completion.

### Certification Auditor

Needs controlled, read-only access to current documents, objective evidence, audit trails, and management review outputs.

### Executive Sponsor

Needs assurance that the management system is effective, risks are visible, obligations are met, and certification progress is measurable.

## Core Value Proposition

Certisphere replaces fragmented management system tooling with a single governed platform where organisations can define processes, control documentation, collect evidence, run audits, manage risk, train people, and prepare for certification.

## Competitive Differentiators

- Integrated management system model rather than isolated document storage.
- AI suggestions constrained by human review and approval.
- Evidence and audit readiness built into everyday workflows.
- Multi-standard architecture for ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 27001, and future standards.
- Consultant, auditor, administrator, and customer portals aligned to their responsibilities.
- Tenant isolation, audit trails, and role-based permissions from the foundation.

## Long-term Vision

Certisphere will become the operating system for management systems: a platform where organisations can design their process architecture, govern controlled information, prove conformance, manage risk, and continuously improve with AI-assisted insight while preserving human accountability.

The long-term product direction is to support complete certification lifecycles, from initial system build to surveillance audits, recertification, supplier assurance, and enterprise-wide integrated management.
